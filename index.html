Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Habit Tracker ("the App") is developed by Aashish Sanjel / Techbitone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is stored, and how it is used.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following directly on your device:

  • Habits, completion history, and streaks
  • Routine schedule entries
  • Activity log entries ("What's Next")
  • Journal entries, including Reflection/Gratitude, Realities/Problems, and daily reflections
  • App settings and preferences

This local data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. You can also enable an optional App Lock, which requires your fingerprint, face, or device PIN/pattern before the App can be opened, in addition to your device's own lock screen.

None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party unless you choose to sign in with a Google account (see below) or export it yourself.

Guest Mode

If you use the App without signing in ("Guest Mode"), none of your data ever leaves your device. There is no cloud sync, and we have no access to it whatsoever.

Google Sign-In and Cloud Sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App receives basic profile information from your Google account (your name, email address, and profile photo) to identify your account within the App.

Once signed in, your habits, routine schedule, activity log, and journal entries are synced to a private, per-account database (Google Firestore) so they're available across your devices. This data is stored under your own authenticated account and is not accessible to other users of the App.

Please note: while this data is encrypted at rest by Google's infrastructure, it is not end-to-end encrypted the way your on-device data is. This means it is technically accessible to us as the developer through our Firestore console, in the same way it would be for any app using a standard cloud database. We do not access, read, or share this data except as needed to maintain or troubleshoot the service.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Data Export and Reports

You may export your habit data, or download a Daily Report combining your Routine, What's Next log, and Journal entries as a PDF, at any time from within the App. These files are saved to your own device and are fully within your control. Please note that exported files and PDFs are not encrypted - if you choose to share or store them elsewhere, treat them with the same care you would any personal document.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all of your data, both on your device and in the cloud, at any time using "Reset All Data" in the App's Profile settings. This action is irreversible.

If you would like your account itself removed, contact us at the email below and we will process your request promptly.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
