Habit Tracker ("the App") is developed by Aashish Sanjel / Techbitone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores your habits, completion history, and settings directly on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party unless you choose to export it yourself.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App receives basic profile information from your Google account, including your name, email address, and profile photo, solely to identify your account within the App.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage data. The App does not sell or share your information with third parties.

Data Export

You may export your habit data at any time from within the App. This file is saved to your own device and is fully within your control.
